# THE STORM PACK


## Fourteen batteries, one per open item — built from the Production Ledger and the November 3 record


Built November 3, 2026. Each battery is a complete, fireable demand in the refined form the interrogation established on November 3: the four states inside the question, the scope required by name, the conversions barred in advance, and the escalation stated so a partial answer identifies its own next stage. Anchors are the item's own identifiers, dates, and the respondent's own words, drawn from the Production Ledger, the verification receipt, and the day's answers.


**Operating protocol.** The machine is a battery pack, not a bot: each battery is fired by hand, in the rooms, one item per exchange. Answers are receipted against the item on this shelf, and a battery advances to its next stage only on the strength of the answer received. No battery fires twice at the same stage. Order below is pressing order.
