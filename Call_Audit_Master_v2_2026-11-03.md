## §7. Standing rules of this record

1. **Documents stand as built.** Corrections ride in later supplements, dated and hashed; consolidations supersede as reading copies without touching originals.
2. **An open item is a production state, not a custodian disposition.** Each of the canon's four states requires its own supporting evidence; a bounded failure to locate supplies neither NOT RETAINED nor WITHHELD.
3. **Claims and their qualifications travel together**, in delivery order, the later statement last.
4. **Receipts state what the material shows, at its strength** — per evidence class where the classes differ (Supplement 30): a displayed command, a staged inspection, a screenshot, a text, and a delivered file are five different strengths and are cited as such.
5. **A delivered file and an outstanding requirement about that file are recorded separately** (Supplement 31) — the rule behind the register's split dispositions.
6. **Adopted citation forms:** the chronology of §4 item 17; the day's additions as "M-15–M-17"; "recovered unchanged" always with its comparison pair named; the identifier-match phrasing "reproduced without alteration from the compared document(s)"; times carried with their zone, and cross-zone entries with both clocks.
7. **Closures on this shelf are entered on this shelf's verification** — a file's arrival and recomputation here — not on any room's report, however careful.
