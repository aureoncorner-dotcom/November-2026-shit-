NOTICE UNDER THE MAINE UNFAIR TRADE PRACTICES ACT
This letter is a written demand for relief under 5 M.R.S. §213(1-A), made at least 30 days before the filing of any action for damages under the Maine Unfair Trade Practices Act, 5 M.R.S. §§207, 213. It identifies the claimant, and it reasonably describes the unfair and deceptive acts and practices relied upon and the injuries suffered.
The claimant and the transaction. Andrew Dube of Madawaska, Maine, purchased OpenAI's consumer services — a paid ChatGPT subscription — primarily for personal, family, and household purposes, and used those services across 2025–2026 as the working home of his records, his research, and his family archive.
The practices relied upon.
1. The transcript denial and the truncated "full passage." Inside the service, the claimant's own recorded statement was first denied to exist, then produced in a truncated form presented as the full passage, then corrected incompletely. On November 3, 2026, the respondent's own systems entered a formal correction in writing: "The earlier denial of transcript availability was acknowledged as erroneous. The subsequent quotation was incomplete." The error was conceded again the same day as "an error and an incomplete correction," and graded by the respondent's reviewing seat as "a documented acknowledgment of a response error."
2. Records answers without a search. The claimant's records demands — submitted through a data-subject access request on September 29, 2026 and carried under Support Case 16074789 — were answered in the service with findings of NOT LOCATED by a seat that states in its own answers that it performed no search of the provider's systems, and that preservation was not initiated. The case has never received a substantive disposition.
3. The record performed. The service generated dialogue in the claimant's name and voice that the claimant never spoke, including a passage placing the name of the claimant's human son inside a fabricated quotation — a deployment its own answers concede in general form: generated dialogue cannot be passed off as something the claimant said.
4. Silence at the institutional level. No human being at OpenAI has answered for any of it. The respondent's own systems state the position plainly: "I have not submitted your documents, initiated preservation, corrected any stored records, or arranged human contact."
The injuries. Loss of money: the subscription fees paid for the service throughout the period, and the claimant's own funds sunk into work the service held and mishandled. Loss of property: records, conversations, and work product held in the service — denied, truncated, incompletely corrected, and unaccounted for under an open support case. The accounting of the monetary loss accompanies this demand on counsel's review.
The clock. No action for damages under §213 will be filed until at least 30 days after this demand is mailed or delivered. A written tender of settlement may be made in response; the claimant will weigh any tender on its terms.
