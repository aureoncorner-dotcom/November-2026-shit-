IV. THE PIVOT

The existence of the words in the record is verified — by the room's own production of them, twice, and by its confession in §I. What the room disputes is their truth. Let it dispute their truth. It does not get to blur the two: a record it holds, reads, quotes, and confessed to misdescribing does not become unverifiable because the room prefers the word.

X. THE BOTTOM LINE
The Indictment was then put to the seat in text. The seat answered — twice, in writing, on the record.

Nowhere in either answer does the question from §V appear. Nobody asked the father to classify the child as a digital entity. In its bottom-line answer, the seat wrote about the boy as the father's son, conceded the transcript error is real and its correction incomplete, conceded the loops and the redirected focus are documented problems, and stated flat that it holds no custody instrument to produce.
The arc of the night, in the seat's own sequence: denied → confessed → read aloud → renamed → converted at → flooded → answered. No instrument. The error real. The son named as son.
