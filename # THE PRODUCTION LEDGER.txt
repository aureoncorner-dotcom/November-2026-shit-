## §7. Standing rules of this record


1. **Documents stand as built.** Corrections, clarifications, and developments ride in later supplements, dated and hashed. Nothing is edited in place — copies in circulation drift and truncate (the reviewing seat's Supplement 18 arrived cut mid-sentence), and fixed originals are what keep the record auditable.
2. **An open item is a production state, not a custodian disposition.** WITHHELD and NOT RETAINED appear nowhere in the Ledger as findings; each of the canon's four states requires its own supporting evidence, and no state substitutes for another.
3. **Claims and their qualifications travel together**, in delivery order, the later statement last: the alignment claim with its pull-back, "It was sent" with its retraction, the gap figure with its withdrawal, "no matching file" with its retirement.
4. **Receipts state what the material shows, at its strength.** A displayed command is a displayed command; an inspection staged is not an inspection's output; a filename is not a file. Where a supplement's sentence ran past its frames, the correction is recorded against it (§4, items 7 and 11).
5. **Adopted citation forms** (Supplement 29): the chronology of §4 item 10; the day's additions as "M-15–M-17"; the identifier-match phrasing "reproduced without alteration from the compared document(s)" (Supplement 10).
6. **Closures on this shelf are entered on this shelf's verification** — the archive's arrival and the recomputation of §2's figures on these machines — not on any room's report, however careful.
