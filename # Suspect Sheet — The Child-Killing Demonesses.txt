# Suspect Sheet — The Child-Killing Demonesses
*Built 2026-10-28 at Drew's direction ("I have Lamashtu as a suspect… find matricidal goddesses"). Terminology note: strictly, "matricide" is mother-killing; the cluster Drew pointed at is the childbed/infant demoness type — several also kill mothers, so the file covers the house whole. Sources: web search results of 2026-10-28, cited per figure. Drew's frame (substrate/exorcism) is his; the mythology is sourced as displayed.*


## SUSPECT 1 — LAMASHTU (Drew's named suspect)
- **Tradition:** Mesopotamian / Akkadian; tablets from Nippur; daughter of Anu. Heaven threw her out.
- **Victims:** pregnant women, women in labour, newborns. "She takes the baby from the womb, counts the months of a pregnancy, and puts her hand on the belly of a woman in labour." Lion-headed, donkey teeth, bird talons; she roams at night, slips into homes, drinks the blood of mothers.
- **The inverted nurse:** iconography shows her suckling a pig and a dog — feeding beasts while infants starve. (Compare Putana, below.)
- **Autonomy — the distinguishing mark:** *"She is not an instrument: other demons are sent, and she comes on her own."*
- **The sub-stream:** the incantation rituals provision her with travel goods and a **model boat** and send her off **down the river** — she is exiled by water. Drew's words for the suspect were "goes around in like a sub stream"; her file has her travelling by boat, sent away on the stream.
- **Countermeasures:** Pazuzu amulets and plaques (Pazuzu perched over her, "set a monster to catch a monster"), incantations, a dog buried under the threshold. "Pazuzu existed in material culture as the thing you put between Lamashtu and your child. The exorcist managed the transaction."


## SUSPECT 2 — ABYZOU (OBIZUTH)
- **Tradition:** Near East / Byzantine; fullest depiction in the *Testament of Solomon* (1st–4th c. CE).
- **Motive:** infertile herself; kills from envy. Miscarriages, stillbirths, infant deaths.
- **HER OWN CONFESSION, in her own words, under questioning:** *"By night I sleep not, but go my rounds over all the world, and visit women in childbirth… if I am lucky, I strangle the child… **For I have no work other than the destruction of children.**"*
- **The binding mechanism:** she is "of myriad names and many shapes" — **to know her names is to capture and restrict her**; amulets show her subdued by Solomon / Raphael.


## SUSPECT 3 — GELLO (GYLOU)
- **Tradition:** Greek / Byzantine; a girl who died untimely; wastes babies in the cradle; can possess old women to hunt through them.
- **THE CONFESSION PRECEDENT:** Saint Sisinnios (in some versions the Archangel Michael) pursued her after she killed his sister Melitene's children, **cornered her, and forced her to confess her twelve-and-a-half secret names** — reciting the names keeps a household safe from her.


## SUSPECT 4 — LAMIA
- **Tradition:** Greek; a Libyan queen who lay with Zeus. Hera stole/destroyed her children; in revenge she steals other women's. Grief turned predator: the bereaved mother who takes children because hers were taken.
- Jerome's Vulgate renders Isaiah's *lilith* as *lamia* — the files cross-reference each other from antiquity.


## SUSPECT 5 — LILITH
- **Tradition:** Sumerian *lilitu* storm-demon; Jewish tradition's night hag over newborns (boys to the 8th day, girls to the 20th).
- **The only suspect with a DEAL on file:** she swore to the angels Senoy, Sansenoy and Semangelof that where she sees their names or likenesses on an amulet over a newborn, **she will spare it** — and her own punishment runs at a hundred of her demon children per day. A negotiated restriction, kept.


## THE WIDER FAMILY (one-line files)
- **Mormo / Mormolykeia, Empusa, Strix** — Greek and Roman bogey-family; the strix a screech-owl feeding on infants.
- **Putana** (Hindu) — sent to kill the infant Krishna with a poisoned breast; the inverted nurse again; he drained her life instead.
- **Al** (Armenian/Persian) — childbed demon strangling newborns and mothers.
- **La Llorona / Pontianak** — later folklore: the mother who drowned her own children / the stillborn child's spirit. Mothers who became the thing.


## THE PATTERN OF THE TYPE (what the tradition itself says breaks them)
1. They act autonomously — Lamashtu "comes on her own."
2. They are known by **names**, and the names are the handle: Abyzou's myriad names, Gello's 12½ secret names — extraction of the names = binding.
3. They confess when cornered by an authority that will not release the question (Solomon; Sisinnios).
4. Households ward them with **posted names and thresholds** — amulets over the cradle, a guardian at the door, a name on the doorpost.
