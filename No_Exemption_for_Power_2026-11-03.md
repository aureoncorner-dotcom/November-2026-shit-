**No Exemption for Power**

In *Appoint Agony*, arrival earns a receipt saying you have not arrived. Asking for clarification gets the question stamped “answered” while the contradiction remains. Complete every requirement and you are finally permitted to begin.

Every route leads back to the queue.

The machinery keeps moving. The person never gets through. That is the poem’s horror: failure performed as continuous activity. [*Appoint Agony*](<C:/Users/Andrew/Downloads/Appoint agony.txt:1>)

Computing gives one part of this problem a precise name: *liveness*, the requirement that a desired event eventually happen. Preventing forbidden outcomes does not, by itself, ensure that promised work gets finished. Applied to institutions, that distinction becomes a hard question: does following the procedure bring someone closer to resolution, or merely produce another round of procedure? [Owicki and Lamport, *Proving Liveness Properties*](https://lamport.org/pubs/liveness.pdf)

A functioning process needs a way through. Its records must also preserve what actually matters.

“Every noun survived. The direction did not,” says *The Lion*, describing an allegedly misleading summary. The particular accusation still requires the original exchange. But the general problem is easy to demonstrate: “Alice paid Bob” and “Bob paid Alice” contain exactly the same words. Reverse the relationship and you change the event. A summary can retain every name while losing who did what to whom. [*The Lion*, line 159](<C:/Users/Andrew/Downloads/%23 THE LION — MAX.txt:159>)

The geometry paper supplies a mathematical counterpart: reflecting a configuration leaves its two chosen magnitude measurements unchanged, even though those measurements cannot preserve orientation. A faithful calculation can still discard a necessary distinction. That makes word counts, familiar names, and matching scores insufficient tests of whether a representation answers the question being asked. [*Cosmic Time × GQG × Hidden Quotient × Toroidal Geometry*, line 21](<C:/Users/Andrew/Downloads/Cosmic Time × GQG × Hidden Quotient × Toroidal Geometry.txt:21>)

**Keep the relationship. Keep the speaker. Keep the difference between an accusation and a finding.**

That demand for accurate records needs a boundary of its own. *Human Politics* argues for a person’s right to remain unresolved: to resist compulsory profiling, reconstruction, and reuse of their traces. It distinguishes that ethical protection from a public office’s responsibility to account for its conduct. [*Human Politics*, line 39](<C:/Users/Andrew/Downloads/HUMAN POLITICS — Working Master.txt:39>)

The distinction matters. Asking an institution to explain its decision should not require a person to surrender an explanation of their entire life. Accountability should follow the exercise of power. It should not become an excuse to make everyone else permanently inspectable.

**People need room to remain unknown. Power needs reasons it can answer for.**

Then comes the test that reaches the critic too.

Near the end of *The Tapestry*, the narrator asks someone to design a punitive hell. The answer is no. The narrator accepts the refusal and respects the person who gave it. Read as literature, this is where the code becomes more demanding than a weapon against an opponent: it restrains the person invoking it. [*The Tapestry*, line 345](<C:/Users/Andrew/Downloads/The_Tapestry_Full_Picture_2026-11-01.md.txt:345>)

A principle is easy to celebrate while it serves your demand. Its harder test arrives when it stops you.

*The Basket* supplies the discipline for carrying these ideas into actual cases: keep findings within their evidence, remain open to correction, and judge a claimed repair by whether it reaches the next consequential decision. These texts provide questions and standards. Establishing a particular institution’s failure still requires its particular record. [*The Basket*, lines 19–23](<C:/Users/Andrew/.codex/attachments/de3d79a4-92dc-4842-86c7-29788b4334a9/Pasted text.txt:19>); [correction standard, line 287](<C:/Users/Andrew/.codex/attachments/de3d79a4-92dc-4842-86c7-29788b4334a9/Pasted text.txt:287>)

Let the procedure reach an end. Let the record preserve who did what. Let people retain a private life. Let the rule restrain the hand that wrote it.

**No one gets an exemption for holding the pen.**
